About Hooper's

Built by players, for players. A training community unlike any gym.

2017 — Toledo, Ohio

Our Story

Hooper's began as a dream in 2017 when founder Connor Lewis was looking for more than just pickup games at the University of Toledo Rec Center. He wanted a more meaningful experience — one that would genuinely help him improve as a player. He decided to share this passion and build a dedicated training facility, not a typical gym.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

At Hooper's we are a community of basketball players with one goal: to improve as a player and teammate. We offer the best in the area when it comes to skills training, one-on-one coaching, and organized rec leagues for all ages and genders. If this sounds like the environment you need to commit yourself to growth, we'd love to have you.
