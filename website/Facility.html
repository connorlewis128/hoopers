Where We Train

Our Facility

Professional-grade courts and training equipment for every level of player.

Court Access

The Courts

We have two sets of courts. The first set is open for practice, pickup games, and individual training during unscheduled times. The second set is dedicated to league games, coaching sessions, and other scheduled events — ensuring a focused environment for structured play.

Equipment & Gym

Training

We also offer cones, pop-up defenders, shot restrictors, and other skills training equipment to help you sharpen your game. Additionally, we have a fully equipped weight room with machines and free weights for personal use and trainer-led sessions.
